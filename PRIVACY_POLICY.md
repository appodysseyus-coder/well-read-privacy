# Well Read Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 11, 2026  
**App:** Well Read for Android (com.appodyssey.ideas), including Well Read Pro  
**Developer:** App Odyssey  
**Contact:** app.odyssey.us@gmail.com

Well Read gives you bite-size idea cards from books and thinkers, with daily cards, spaced review and listen mode. This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

## The short version

- All cards are built into the app. Well Read makes no network requests.
- Your saved cards, your own cards, streak, review progress and settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
- Well Read has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking code, and App Odyssey runs no server for it.
- We don't sell or share your personal information.

## Information we collect

None. The app doesn't send your reading activity, saved cards, your own cards or settings to App Odyssey, so we have no copy of them.

## Information stored only on your device

- **Your reading:** topics you picked, cards you saved or marked, collections you follow, your streak and spaced-review schedule.
- **Your own cards:** the text of idea cards you write.
- **Your settings:** theme, text size, daily goal, reminder time and reading speed.

You can delete your own cards in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove all of it. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's data in your own device backup, which Google handles under your Google account settings. We can't see those backups.

## Listen mode

Listen mode reads cards aloud using the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for example Speech Services by Google). The app passes the card text to that engine on the phone. The engine is a separate app with its own privacy policy.

## Reminders and widget

The optional daily reminder is scheduled on your phone. On Android 13 and later the app asks for notification permission when you turn the reminder on, and re-schedules the reminder after your phone restarts. The home screen widget shows a card from the app on your phone.

## Sharing and backup files

When you share a card as an image or save a backup file, the app creates the file on your phone and hands it to the app or location you pick. What happens next is up to you and that app.

## Feedback and card reports

**Report** on a card and **Send feedback** in Settings open your own email app with a pre-filled message to app.odyssey.us@gmail.com (the card's title and ID). Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. If you do, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply and fix the card.

## Purchases

Well Read Pro will be sold through Google Play. If you buy it, Google Play handles the payment and we don't receive your payment details; the app only learns whether Pro is active on your Google account. During testing, Pro features are unlocked and nothing is sold.

## Data sale and sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

## Third-party code

The app is built with open-source libraries (Capacitor and Android Jetpack). It includes no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code. Google Play may collect information when you download or update the app, under Google's own privacy policy.

## Children

Well Read is intended for people 13 and older. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Security

Data stored on your phone is protected by Android's app storage protections.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the updated version at this address and change the effective date above.
